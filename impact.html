Our Impact

Every ticket, sponsorship, and bid at Hong Kong After Dark goes directly to UCON's Government Advocacy program: building coalitions, driving political support, and championing the ballot measures and regulations that shape our industry.

Wins for Union Contractors

Recent Advocacy Wins

This is what auction night makes possible. Thanks to your support, our political advocacy has delivered key victories for union contractors across California.

$100B+ in Infrastructure Funding

Secured in federal, state, and local funding to support critical construction projects statewide.

Retention Capped at 5%

On public and private works projects, improving cash flow and reducing financial strain on contractors.

Change Order Payment Protections

Advanced legislation to protect fair and timely payment for change orders on public works projects.

Work Zone Speed Cameras

Authorized cameras in active construction work zones to improve safety for workers.

Defeated Harmful Legislation

Stopped bills that would have significantly exposed contractors to unnecessary fines, penalties, and enforcement risk.

$6M+ for Permitting Backlog

Secured funding to help address the severe permitting backlog at the Bay Area Air District.
